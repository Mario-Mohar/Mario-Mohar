### 🧭 About Me

I came to software through IT operations and QA, and that still shapes how I work: I care about whether something runs reliably, not only whether it demos well. Right now I split my time between two companies:

- 🚀 **Co-Founder & CTO at [Scoutlane](https://scoutlane.at)**, an AI-powered B2B recruiting platform for the DACH market
- 🏢 **Co-Manager at [Syventa](https://www.syventa.at)**, IT consulting and digitalization for SMEs, run together with my wife Iris

I work with AI coding agents every day. My part is deciding what gets built, how it's tested, and running it in production.

MBA and Master's in AI for Business (Universidad Isabel I / ENEB Barcelona). Certified in ISTQB Foundation & AI Testing, AWS Cloud Practitioner, Microsoft Power Platform, and as a DEKRA IT expert (IT-Sachverständiger).

### 📦 Open Source

Most of what I publish comes out of my own workday: something was tedious, so I
built the tool. Two of them you can just click and try.

| Project | What it does | |
|---|---|---|
| **[linkedin-panel](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/linkedin-panel)** | Keeps a history of your own LinkedIn posts and their reach, which LinkedIn itself does not. Local dashboard, read-only, German and English. | `TypeScript` `Playwright` |
| **[omarchy-mail-inbox](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/omarchy-mail-inbox)** | Unread mail from every mailbox behind one icon in the Omarchy bar. Plain IMAP, read and reply without leaving the bar. | `QML` `Python` |
| **[cloud-storage-deduplicator](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/cloud-storage-deduplicator)** | Finds duplicate files in Google Drive and OneDrive by checksum. Dry run by default, moves instead of deleting. | `Python` |
| **[Duplikat-Filesorter](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/Duplikat-Filesorter)** | The same idea for local disks, as a desktop app. Moves duplicates aside and logs every move, so nothing is lost. | `Rust` `Tauri` |
| **[clockify-time-tracker](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/clockify-time-tracker)** | Compares hours booked in Clockify against your contracted working time, including Austrian public holidays. | `SvelteKit` |
| **[pomodoro-focus](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/pomodoro-focus)** | Pomodoro timer with a task list and statistics. Runs entirely in the browser, no account, no server. | `JavaScript` |
| 🎮 **[little-bear-game](https://mario-mohar.github.io/little-bear-game/)** | A platformer: run, double jump, collect coins. **Playable in the browser**, no account needed. | `JavaScript` `Express` |
| 🎮 **[TicTacTo](https://mario-mohar.github.io/TicTacTo/)** | Tic tac toe for two at one screen. **Playable in the browser**, three files and no build step. | `JavaScript` |

Three of my bar widgets are listed and verified in the [Omarchy plugin marketplace](https://omarchyplugins.com): [Mail Inbox](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/omarchy-mail-inbox), [NFL](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/omarchy-nfl) and [Monitor Profiles](https://github.com/Mario-Mohar/omarchy-monitor-profiles).
